# Lords of Doomspire - Rules Change History

## July 19 - Initial Design

- Game created as "Lords of Doomspire"
- 4 players, 60-90 min, D3 dice
- Champions explore an 8x8 island grid with 3 tiers
- Three victory conditions: Combat (defeat dragon, 12-16 might), Diplomatic (10 Fame), Economic (control all starred resource tiles)
- Starting resources: 1 Food, 1 Wood
- Simple turn structure: Roll dice, take actions (Move & Act, Harvest, Build, Boat Travel)
- Auto-claiming resource tiles (no action needed)
- "Market Day" event card mechanic
- Chapel resets fame to 0

## July 19-20 - Early Iteration

- Diplomatic victory threshold raised to 20 Fame, then lowered to 10
- **Gold Victory added** as 4th win condition (10+ Gold)
- Dragon might reduced from 12-16 to 7-9 (6 base + D3)
- Market Day event removed
- Economic victory changed from "all starred tiles" to "3+ starred tiles"
- Detailed components list added (knight inventory cards, cheat sheets, resource tokens)
- Tile tier system and biome card themes formalized (Plains, Woodlands, Mountains)

## July 21-24 - Movement & Action Overhaul

- **Major action economy change**: Once a champion performs any tile interaction, they're done for the turn
- Champions must stop when entering unexplored tiles
- Auto-claiming resource tiles removed (now requires explicit action)
- Multiple dice can be combined for longer movement in a single move
- Boat transport restrictions clarified

## July 25-26 - Combat & Economy Rebalancing

- Knight inventory system formalized (2-item carry limit)
- **Knight-to-knight combat looting added** (steal a resource or item)
- Dragon might changed to 8 + D3
- Victory thresholds adjusted: Fame 12+, Economic 4+ starred tiles, Gold 20+
- Building costs overhauled: Market (3/3 to 2/2), Blacksmith (3/3 to 2/2)
- "Dock" building removed; boats bought directly
- Chapel/Monastery made one-per-player
- Trader reworked: from "buy any resource for 2 gold" to "2:1 resource exchange"
- Temple changed: from "reset fame to 0" to "sacrifice 3 fame for 1 might"
- Chapel renamed to Temple (on the board); Chapel became a castle building

## July 28 - Buildings & Protection

- Chapel and Monastery castle buildings added (3 Fame / 5 Fame)
- **Protection mechanic expanded**: warships now protect coastal tiles in their ocean zone
- Tile conquest mechanic formalized (pay 1 might or 1 fame)
- Knight-to-knight combat loser no longer pays healing cost
- Claiming a tile after winning combat no longer costs an extra die

## July 29-31 - Major Rules Restructure

- **Complete document rewrite** with Part 1 / Part 2 split for teaching
- **Three-phase turn structure**: Roll Phase (parallel) -> Move Phase (sequential) -> Harvest Phase (parallel)
- **Dice tax** introduced: extra dice beyond 2 cost 2 food each
- **Fleeing mechanic** added with D3 roll (fail / partial success / full success)
- Tile interactions split into "Automatic" and "Voluntary"
- Rule: no adventure card after combat ("too exhausted")
- Tier names changed to biome names: Tier 1 = Plains, Tier 2 = Hills, Tier 3 = Mountains
- Build action moved to end of harvest phase (no die required)
- **Combat support bonus increased** from +1 to +2 per supporting unit
- Oasis tiles introduced (adventure tiles that can be restocked)
- Round 1 restriction: no knight-vs-knight combat

## August 2 - Victory System Overhaul (Exile System)

- **"Exile the dragon" system** replaced instant win: Victory Points calculated after dragon is exiled
  - Dragon exile worth 8 VP, plus points for might, fame, knights, tiles, buildings, etc.
  - Final ranking: King, Hand of the King, Head of Forest-Observing, Court Jester
- Allied support from other players announced after dice roll (enabling diplomacy/betrayal)
- Fleeing reworked (partial success = lose 1 resource, flee to owned tile)
- Victory thresholds: Fame 15+, Gold 12+, Economic 4+ starred tiles

## August 2 (later) - Dragon Impression System (replacing VP)

- **Victory Points scrapped**, replaced by Dragon Impression system
- Dragon must be impressed 3 times total (by any players across a shared counter)
- Each impression = take a treasure stack reward, knight flown home
- 3rd impression = game ends, that player becomes King
- Rings introduced as another way to impress the dragon
- Dragon's treasure hoard: 12 resources in 3 stacks placed on Doomspire
- Final ranking: King (last to impress), Hand of the King (most tiles), Master of Coin (most gold), Court Jester
- Dragon combat: both player and dragon roll dice

## August 17-25 - Polish

- Fletcher building added
- Blockade/harvest rules refined
- Starting resources simplified to 2 Gold (home tile provides food/wood)
- Various wording clarifications and minor balance tweaks

## February 25, 2026 - Post-Playtest Overhaul

Based on playtest feedback identifying these problems: turtling too safe, PVP not rewarding enough, fame not relevant enough, too easy to instant-win, losing players need more catch-up options.

- **Per-player dragon impression track**: Each player now has their own impression track (0-3). First to 3 wins. Replaces the shared global counter. Knights stay at Doomspire after impressing (no longer flown home). Must continue impressing each round while at Doomspire or be eaten.
- **PVP loot scaling**: Winner steals 1 item + resources equal to half the loser's resource tile count (rounded up). Previously was just 1 resource or 1 item.
- **Conquer/Bribe tile takeover**: Removed "Incite Revolt" (1 fame to unclaim) and "Conquer" (1 might to take over). Replaced with two options: Conquer (2 fame) or Bribe (2 gold).
- **Combat support includes diagonals**: Support range expanded from 4 orthogonal tiles to all 8 surrounding tiles. Tile protection remains orthogonal only.
- **Doomspire PVP**: If an opposing knight is at Doomspire, you fight them first, then face the dragon. Other players can support the dragon in combat.
