# Fate Cards - Draft

## Overview

A deck of ~26 fate cards drawn once per round during the dice roll phase. The top card of the deck is always face-up, so all players can see what's coming next round.

**When**: At the start of the dice roll phase (before rolling), resolve the face-up fate card. Then flip the next card face-up.

**Starts**: Round 3 (flip the first card face-up at the start of round 2, so players see it coming).

## Card Types

- **Council Vote** (~11 cards): Fame-weighted voting. Creates player interaction, alliances, and catch-up mechanics.
- **Event** (~9 cards): Automatic effects that change the rules for one round. No decisions needed.
- **Gathering** (~3 cards): Vote to move all knights to a special location for a shared benefit. Knights stay there until moved next round.
- **Minor** (~3 cards): Small bonuses or redistribution.

## Voting Rules

Council vote and gathering cards trigger a vote. Two formats:

**YES/NO vote**: Each player votes yes or no. Multiply each vote by that player's fame. If total YES fame > total NO fame, the effect passes. Tie or NO majority = no effect.

**TARGET vote**: Each player votes for another player (not themselves). Multiply each vote by that player's fame. The player with the most fame-weighted votes against them is the target. Tie = no one is affected.

## Council Vote Cards (10)

### 1. Tax the Crown

*"The lords convene to discuss the hoarding of wealth."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: The player with the most gold must pay 4 gold. They choose how to distribute it among the other players. If tied for most gold, all tied players pay 2 gold each and distribute it.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 2. Land Reform

*"Whispers of inequality spread across the island."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: The player with the most resource tiles must give 1 tile (their choice, not home tile) to any player of their choosing. If tied for most tiles, all tied players give a tile.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 3. Disarmament

*"The council calls for a show of restraint."*

**Vote**: TARGET

Vote for a player. That player loses 2 might (minimum 0).

**Tie**: No one is affected.

---

### 4. Banishment

*"The lords have grown weary of a rival's presence."*

**Vote**: TARGET

Vote for a player. All of that player's knights are sent home.

**Tie**: No one is affected.

---

### 5. Charity Decree

*"The people demand aid for the struggling."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: Each player (except those tied for fewest total resources) gives 1 resource of their choice to one of the players with the fewest total resources.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 6. Famine

*"The crops have withered. Should we ration?"*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: No player may harvest this round.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 7. War Tax

*"Military buildup must be funded."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: Every player with 3+ might must pay 2 gold or lose 1 might. Players with less than 3 might are unaffected.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 8. Harvest Ban

*"The council targets a lord's economy."*

**Vote**: TARGET

Vote for a player. That player cannot harvest this round.

**Tie**: No one is affected.

---

### 9. Lockdown

*"The council restricts a lord's movements."*

**Vote**: TARGET

Vote for a player. That player cannot move any knights this round (boats and harvesting still allowed).

**Tie**: No one is affected.

---

### 10. Penalty

*"The council imposes a burden."*

**Vote**: TARGET

Vote for a player. That player rolls one fewer die this round (minimum 1).

**Tie**: No one is affected.

---

## Gathering Cards (3)

These are YES/NO votes. If passed, all knights on the board are moved to the specified special tile. Knights stay there and must move from that location next round. Each player gets the gathering's benefit.

### 12. Festival

*"A grand festival is held at the trader's bazaar!"*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: All knights move to the Trader tile. Each player may make one free trade at 1:1 rate (any resource for any resource).

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 13. Grand Tourney

*"The lords are called to prove their strength!"*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: All knights move to the Mercenary Camp tile. A tournament is held: all players roll 1D3. The highest roller gains 1 might. Ties reroll among tied players.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 14. Prayer Day

*"The faithful gather to seek divine guidance."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: All knights move to the Temple tile. All players gain 1 fame.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

### 11. Fame Reversal

*"The mighty are humbled, the humble are lifted."*

**Vote**: YES/NO

**If passed**: The player with the most fame loses 2 fame. The player with the least fame gains 2 fame. If tied for most, all tied players lose 2. If tied for least, all tied players gain 2.

**Tie**: No effect.

---

## Event Cards (9)

### 15. Bountiful Harvest

*"The land yields abundantly this season."*

All harvests produce double resources this round.

---

### 16. Ceasefire

*"An uneasy peace settles over the island."*

No PVP combat this round. Knights may pass through each other freely without being stopped.

---

### 17. Fog of War

*"A thick fog blankets the coastline."*

No boat movement or transport this round.

---

### 18. Dragon's Shadow

*"The dragon circles the island, casting fear."*

All knights within 2 tiles of Doomspire (Manhattan distance) must flee to their home tile. Knights already at Doomspire are unaffected.

---

### 19. Oasis Bloom

*"Springs bubble up from the earth."*

All oasis tiles and adventure tiles with 0 remaining tokens gain +1 adventure token.

---

### 20. Storm Season

*"Fierce storms batter the coast."*

All boats are pushed: move each boat 1 ocean zone clockwise. Boats cannot transport knights this round.

---

### 21. Merchant Fair

*"Traveling merchants flood the markets."*

All trader exchanges are 1:1 instead of 2:1 this round. All building costs are reduced by 1 of each resource type (minimum 1 each).

---

### 22. Dragon Sleeping

*"The dragon slumbers deeply, snoring like thunder."*

The dragon cannot be impressed by fame, gold, economy, or rings this round -- only by combat. However, the sleeping dragon's might is reduced by 2 for this round.

---

### 23. Dragon Off Hunting

*"The dragon has left Doomspire to hunt in distant lands."*

The dragon is absent this round. Knights at Doomspire cannot impress the dragon, but may take a treasure stack (if any remain). Normal PVP rules apply at Doomspire -- if multiple players want treasure, they'll have to fight for it.

---

## Minor Cards (3)

### 24. Favorable Winds

*"A gentle breeze fills the sails."*

All boat movements get +1 step this round.

---

### 25. The Gift

*"A gesture of goodwill... or obligation."*

Starting from the player with the most fame, each player passes 1 resource of their choice to the player on their left. Continue clockwise until all players have given and received.

---

### 26. Fortune Smiles

*"Luck favors the desperate."*

The player with the fewest total resources gains 3 resources of their choice.

---

## Design Notes

- **Council votes naturally create catch-up**: Tax the Crown, Land Reform, Charity Decree all redistribute from strong to weak. Three weaker players can outvote one leader.
- **Fame is political power**: High fame = more votes. This makes fame valuable throughout the game, not just at Doomspire.
- **Targeted vs. blanket votes create different dynamics**: TARGET cards let the majority punish one player. YES/NO blanket cards (Famine, gatherings) create "this hurts you more than me" asymmetric tradeoffs.
- **Gatherings are board resets**: Moving all knights to one location disrupts camping strategies, creates new positions to play from, and can pull knights away from Doomspire.
- **Dragon cards add Doomspire drama**: Dragon Sleeping rewards combat builds, Dragon Off Hunting creates a treasure race with PVP at Doomspire.
- **Face-up preview**: Players always see what's coming and can plan, negotiate, and lobby before the vote happens.
- **Deck composition**: ~42% council votes (interactive), ~35% events (game-changing), ~12% gatherings (dramatic), ~12% minor (breather).
